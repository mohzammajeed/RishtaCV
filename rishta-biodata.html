What is a rishta biodata?

A rishta biodata, also called a rishta CV or marriage proposal biodata, is a one-page summary of a person that families share when they look for a match. It tells the other side who you are, what you do and what kind of family you come from, so both sides can decide quickly whether to talk further.

What to include in a marriage proposal biodata

  • Personal details: full name, age or date of birth, height, marital status, city.
  • Education and profession: highest degree, institution, current job or business, and income range if you are comfortable sharing it.
  • Family background: parents' occupation, number of brothers and sisters, caste or maslak if it matters to you.
  • What you are looking for: two or three lines about the kind of partner and family you hope for.
  • A clear photo: front-facing with a plain background.
  • Contact: a phone number or WhatsApp that a parent or guardian answers.

Tips for a good rishta CV

  • Keep it honest and short. One page is enough.
  • Use a recent, clear photo, and only photos of people who agreed to appear.
  • Share your phone number only with families you are serious about.
  • Update the CV when your job, education or city changes.
