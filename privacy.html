Privacy Policy

RishtaCV is owned and run by Mohzam Majeed Sheikh. Contact: creatormms720@gmail.com. Last updated: 9 November 2026.

What we collect

  • Your account details: name and email (from Google or from signing up).
  • The CV details you type: name, age, date of birth, profession, city, address, phone number, email, family and income details, and for a Job CV your work experience, education and skills, and your photo.
  • Payment screenshots you upload to unlock a CV (these may show your name and phone number).
  • Basic technical data kept by our hosting providers.

Why we use it

To create and save your CV, let you download or share it, and to check payments.

Where it is stored

Your login, CVs, photos and screenshots are stored with Google Firebase. The website and server run on Vercel. CV videos are created on your own device and are not uploaded to us.

To check a payment, the screenshot you upload is read automatically on our own server with open-source text recognition (OCR) software, which reads the amount, transaction ID, time and receiver. It is not sent to any outside AI service.

Who can see it

  • Only you can see your CVs, unless you press Share, which creates a link. Anyone who has that link can view that CV, so share it only with people you trust.
  • Payment screenshots are seen only by the owner, to verify payments.
  • We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

  • Payment screenshots are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • CVs are kept until you delete them. Deleting a CV also removes its share link.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your CVs in the app. To delete your account and data, email us at the address above.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no online service is 100% secure. Share links carefully and only with people you trust.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
