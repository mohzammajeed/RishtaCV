Rishta safety tips: protect yourself and your family

Looking for a match online should feel safe. These steps help you protect your details, spot scams and meet with confidence.

Protect your personal details

  • Put a phone or WhatsApp number on your CV that a parent or guardian answers.
  • Leave your CNIC number, bank details and exact home address off the CV. A city is enough at the start.
  • Share photos only with families you are seriously considering.
  • In RishtaCV your CV is private unless you press Share. Delete it from My CVs when you are done.

Watch for these warning signs

  • Someone asks you to pay a fee, send money or recharge a wallet to "confirm" or "book" a rishta.
  • A profile with perfect photos that refuses a video call or a meeting with family.
  • Promises of a visa, a foreign job or quick wealth tied to the marriage.
  • Pressure to decide fast, or to keep the conversation secret from your family.
  • Stories that change, or details that do not match between the CV and what you are told.

Verify before you trust

  • Ask for an introduction through relatives, neighbours or colleagues who know the family.
  • Check education and job details through people you trust, not only through the person who sent the CV.
  • Run a reverse image search on the photo to see if it appears under another name.
  • Have a video call with family present before any meeting.

Meeting safely

  • Meet in a public place or at home with family present, during the day.
  • Tell a trusted person where you are going and when you will be back.
  • Leave if anything feels wrong. You do not owe anyone an explanation.

Using a marriage bureau

Choose a bureau that is known to people you trust. Ask for its terms and fees in writing, take a receipt for anything you pay, and be cautious about large upfront fees.

If something goes wrong

  • Stop contact, do not send money, and keep screenshots of messages and payment details.
  • Report online fraud to Pakistan's cybercrime authorities. The cyber helpline is 1991; check the official website for the current reporting channels.
  • For harassment or misuse of your photos, the Digital Rights Foundation runs a free helpline at 0800-39393.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to put my phone number on a rishta CV?

Only share a number that a parent or guardian answers, and only with families you are serious about. Keep your full home address and CNIC number off the CV.

Should I put my photo on a rishta CV?

Share photos only with families you are seriously considering. If you share a CV link or file, tell the family not to forward it, and delete the CV when you no longer need it.

What should I do if someone asks for money to confirm a rishta?

Stop contact and do not send anything. Real families do not charge advance fees to confirm a match. Keep the messages as proof and report it.

Does RishtaCV charge for rishta matches?

No. RishtaCV does not arrange or confirm rishtas. It only charges Rs.150 to unlock your own CV in HD without a watermark.
