What a good CV format looks like

Most employers skim a CV in under a minute, so the order matters. The most widely used layout is reverse-chronological: your latest job first, then earlier ones.

  1. Name and contact: phone, email and city. Add a LinkedIn link if you have one.
  2. Professional summary: two or three lines on who you are and what you do best.
  3. Work experience: job title, company, dates, and two or three results per role.
  4. Education: degree, institution and year.
  5. Skills and languages: the tools and languages that match the job ad.
  6. Certifications: short courses and licences that support the role.

Tips for a job CV in Pakistan

  • Keep it to one page, or two if you have many years of experience.
  • Start each point with an action word and add numbers where you can, such as "managed a team of 6".
  • Change the summary and skills to match each job ad.
  • A photo is optional; check the job ad, as some employers ask for one.
  • Save and send it as a PDF so the layout does not change.
