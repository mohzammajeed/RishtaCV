About RishtaCV

RishtaCV is a simple tool for making a rishta (matrimonial) CV. It is only for rishta profiles, not job CVs.

  • Choose an Islamic design (Bismillah, green and gold) or a Normal professional design.
  • Fill in your details with easy dropdowns, or write your own answer.
  • Use English or Urdu.
  • Download your CV as PDF, JPG or PNG, or share it with a private link.

Each CV can be unlocked for HD, watermark-free download with a one-time payment of Rs.150 per CV.

RishtaCV is owned and run by Mohzam Majeed Sheikh. Questions? See the Contact page.
