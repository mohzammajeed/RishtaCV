About RishtaCV

RishtaCV is a simple online rishta app for making a rishta (matrimonial) CV or marriage proposal biodata. It also has a separate Job CV design with a clean CV format and CV template for job applications in Pakistan. RishtaCV is not a marriage bureau; it helps you prepare the CV you send to family, a marriage bureau or rishta walay.

  • Choose from four designs: Islamic (Bismillah, green and gold), Normal professional, Biodata (cream and maroon, two columns), or Job CV (a resume for job applications).
  • Fill in your details with easy dropdowns, or write your own answer. A short introduction is written for you, and you can add more to it.
  • Use English or Urdu.
  • Download your CV as PDF or PNG, or share it on WhatsApp with a link.
  • Create a 15-second MP4 video of your CV for WhatsApp Status, Instagram or TikTok.

Guides: how to make a rishta biodata and how to make a job CV for Pakistan.

Each CV can be unlocked for HD, watermark-free download (including the video) with a one-time payment of Rs.150 per CV.
